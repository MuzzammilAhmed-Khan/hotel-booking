Our Story

Fifteen years of thoughtful hospitality

Alloy Grand Hotel opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: luxury doesn't have to feel distant. Since then, we've welcomed travelers from over 60 countries, hosted milestone celebrations, and become a trusted home base for business and leisure guests alike in the heart of the downtown district.

Every room is designed with the same care — quality linens, quiet climate control, and views that remind you exactly where you are. Our team, many of whom have been with us since opening, brings a level of attentiveness that's hard to find anywhere else.

Location

Steps from everything worth seeing

Perched on Skyline Avenue, Alloy Grand puts you within walking distance of the financial district, riverside promenade, and the city's best galleries and restaurants. Public transit and the airport express line are both a five-minute walk away.
