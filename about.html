Our Story

Fifteen years of thoughtful hospitality

Alloy Grand Hotel opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: luxury doesn't have to feel distant. Since then, we've welcomed travelers from over 60 countries, hosted milestone celebrations, and become a trusted home base for business and leisure guests alike in the heart of Banjara Hills.

Every room is designed with the same care — quality linens, quiet climate control, and views that remind you exactly where you are. Our team, many of whom have been with us since opening, brings a level of attentiveness that's hard to find anywhere else.

Location

Steps from everything worth seeing

Perched in Banjara Hills, Alloy Grand puts you within easy reach of HITEC City's business district, the boutiques and cafés of Road No. 36, and the walking trail around Hussain Sagar Lake. Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is a comfortable drive away.
